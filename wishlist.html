How a card gets on this list

One way: a deck's buy block asks for more copies than the collection holds. Buy is the shortfall, so a card the binders already cover is not here at all.

Cards nobody owns but no deck has asked for are not on this page. Most of them are a shortlist rather than a shopping list — the energy-acceleration survey alone seeded 35 candidates into the pipeline to be read and compared. The reason each one was added lives in the note column of wanted-cards.tsv.

Need adds up across decks. Four of a card in one deck and four in another is eight, because both decks stay sleeved and get played against each other. It is not a mistake that a shared staple wants more copies than any one list does.
