# Robot Swords

### Doublade swings for 60 a sword · Steven's Metagross ex loads two Energy a turn, every turn · two robots on the Bench

> [!NOTE]
> **What this file is for.**
>
> Fox's Doublade deck, the X-Boot build, which Xero is testing on [TCG Live](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/tcgl/) before Fox sleeves it. Every card carries regulation mark H, I, or J. Its twin is [Dancing Swords](./dancing-swords.md), the same swords on Metang's *Metal Maker* instead.
>
> The attacker is Doublade, and *Weaponized Swords* counts the Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash in your hand. This build trades Metang's single-Prize engine for Steven's Metagross ex: two guaranteed Energy a turn, and a 340 HP attacker that doesn't care what your hand holds, at the price of a second two-Prize Pokémon. Steven's Metagross ex is also the engine of Fox's [Steel Wolves](./steel-wolves.md). The deck list and the cards come first, then [The Thesis](#the-thesis) and [The Hard Rules](#the-hard-rules), which are how to play it. Game words are defined in [the table rules](./rules.md).

**21 + 28 + 11 = 60.** ✓ Ten Basics, ten Supporters, twelve swords, one ACE SPEC, and nothing over four copies.

**Ten Basics is a 26% mulligan.** Going first, a Steven's Beldum, Poffin, or Poke Pad shows up by the end of your first turn 71% of the time, and a Rare Candy or a Petrel to find one is in your first nine cards 49% of the time. So X-Boot is running on turn two in about half your games and on turn three in most of the rest, through the Steven's Metang. **Genesect ex or a Dawn is in your first nine cards only 57% of the time**; see [Test and Tune](#test-and-tune). About one opening hand in five (22%) holds no Energy.

# Pokémon

### Honedge

**70 HP Basic, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 2.** *Cut* does 10 and you will never use it.

**Four copies, two jobs.** It is the bottom of the line, and it is a sword while it sits in your hand. Every Honedge on the Bench is 60 off this turn's swing, so turn one benches two (one to evolve in the Active Spot, one behind it) and the rest stay in hand. Poffin and Poke Pad find it, and Dawn takes it as the Basic.

### Doublade

**Stage 1, 100 HP, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 2.** The Mega Evolution promo 067, Yukihiro Tada's art, mark J. It is the same card as Perfect Order 057.

***Weaponized Swords* costs [C][C].** Reveal any number of Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash from your hand, and it does 60 for each one. **Revealed, not discarded.** The cards go back into your hand and swing again next turn.

**Four copies.** One is the attacker, one waits in hand for the next Honedge, and the rest are swords. Its cost is all Colorless, so X-Boot's Psychic Energy pays for it as well as the Metal does.

**100 HP means it dies,** and that is the plan, because it is one Prize. Everything attached to it goes to the discard with it.

### Aegislash

**Stage 2, 150 HP, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 3.**

***Slash* does 80 for [C][C][C]. *Metal Slash* does 230 for [M][C][C][C], and this Pokémon can't attack during your next turn.**

**Four copies, and in most games none of them touches the board.** It is the purest sword, so *Metallic Signal* takes it first. It comes out when three swords or fewer are left, or after Judge or Unfair Stamp; [Who attacks](#who-attacks) has the rule. A Doublade that already holds two Energy evolves, takes two more from X-Boot, and has the four *Metal Slash* needs without a card from your hand.

### Steven's Beldum

**70 HP Basic, Retreat 1.** "Steven's" is part of the name, so it evolves only into Steven's Metang, and Rare Candy takes it only to Steven's Metagross ex.

**Three copies.** One is the Candy target, which has to sit a turn before the Candy. The others are spares for a prized Candy or a dragged-up Beldum. Poffin and Poke Pad find it.

### Steven's Metang

**Stage 1, 100 HP, Retreat 2, and no Ability.** It is the slow road to Steven's Metagross ex when no Candy shows: Beldum on turn one, Metang on turn two, Metagross on turn three. One copy. Signal and Poke Pad find it. Its *Metal Slash* locks itself out, and you will never use it.

### Steven's Metagross ex

**The engine.** Stage 2, 340 HP, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 3, two Prizes.

***X-Boot*: once during your turn, search your deck for a Basic Psychic Energy, a Basic Metal Energy, or one of each, and attach them to your Psychic Pokémon and Metal Pokémon in any way you like.**

- **It is two Energy a turn, guaranteed,** as long as the deck still holds one of each type. Two is exactly a fresh Doublade.
- **It reaches any of your Pokémon,** so it loads the backup Honedge, the Active Doublade, or an Aegislash.
- **It searches the deck,** so the Energy Recyclers are what keep it fed.

***Metal Stomp* does 200 for [M][C][C].** That is a swing that ignores your hand entirely, from a 340 HP body that most single hits in the format can't Knock Out.

**Two copies, one in play.** It is two Prizes, so a second one is two more in the open for an engine you already have. Genesect ex's Signal finds it, and so do Dawn and Hilda.

### Genesect ex

**220 HP Basic, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 2, two Prizes.** Black Bolt 067.

***Metallic Signal*: once during your turn, search your deck for up to 2 Evolution Metal Pokémon and put them into your hand.** Doublade and Aegislash both count, so it is +120 damage every turn. Steven's Metang and Steven's Metagross ex count too, which makes Signal the fastest way to the Candy's target.

**Two copies, one in play.** Beside a Metagross ex, it makes four Prizes on the Bench in two bodies, and that is the ceiling. Signal searches the deck, never the hand or the discard, so [Sacred Ash](#sacred-ash) is what refills it. *Protect Charge* does 150 for [M][M][C], an emergency swing only.

### Psyduck

**70 HP Basic, Lightning Weakness, Retreat 1.** Ascended Heroes 039.

***Damp*: Pokémon in play lose any Ability that requires the Pokémon using it to Knock Out itself.** That is Dusknoir's and Dusclops's *Cursed Blast*.

**One copy.** It is the tenth Basic and the Dusknoir answer. Bench it only when they show a Duskull; otherwise it is a pivot.

# Trainers — Supporters

### Dawn

**It searches the deck for a Basic, a Stage 1, and a Stage 2 Pokémon.** Three copies, and the best card in the deck.

| Board | Dawn takes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Turn one going second | Genesect ex, Doublade, Steven's Metagross ex |
| A Steven's Beldum that has sat, no Candy target in hand | Honedge, Doublade, Steven's Metagross ex |
| Everything already up | Honedge, Doublade, Aegislash, for +180 |

### Lana's Aid

**It puts up to three Pokémon without a Rule Box or Basic Energy from the discard into your hand.** Two copies. Every Doublade that dies puts two swords in the discard, and Lana's brings three back. It can't take Genesect ex or Steven's Metagross ex.

### Boss's Orders

**It switches one of their Benched Pokémon into the Active Spot.** Two copies.

- **Early, take the cradle.** Two swords are 120, which kills Riolu, Dreepy, Drakloak, Drilbur, and Gastly.
- **Late, take the Mega that retreated,** the turn you hold six swords.
- **On a short-sword turn, take their engine:** Toxtricity, Meganium, or a Cynthia's Roserade, at three swords each.

> [!WARNING]
> **Leave a Mega Lucario ex in the Active Spot the turn after it used *Mega Brave*.** It can't use *Mega Brave* again that turn, and sending it to the Bench resets it.

### Team Rocket's Petrel

**It searches the deck for a Trainer card.** Two copies. It is the second and third Rare Candy, the second Max Rod and Sacred Ash, and the Stadium toolbox. Supporters it finds wait for next turn.

### Hilda

**It searches the deck for an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy card.** One copy. It takes Steven's Metagross ex and an Energy for the Candy turn, or an Aegislash and a Metal after the hand is shuffled away.

# Trainers — Items

### Buddy-Buddy Poffin

**Two Basic Pokémon with 70 HP or less, straight to the Bench.** Three copies. Honedge, Steven's Beldum, and Psyduck qualify. It is a turn-one card; late in the game it benches swords instead of drawing them.

### Rare Candy

**Two copies.** Steven's Beldum to Steven's Metagross ex, skipping the Metang. Two timing rules decide openings: never on your first turn, and never on a Beldum that arrived this turn. **Search first, then Candy.** Petrel finds a third.

### Poke Pad

**It searches the deck for a Pokémon without a Rule Box.** Two copies, printed Poké Pad. Any sword, a Steven's Beldum, or the Steven's Metang. Never an ex.

### Night Stretcher

**It puts a Pokémon or a Basic Energy from the discard into your hand.** One copy. It is the cheap way back for a Genesect ex or a Steven's Metagross ex, which Lana's can't touch.

### Sacred Ash

**It shuffles up to five Pokémon from the discard into the deck.** One copy. It is the only card that refills what Signal, Dawn, and Poke Pad search. **Play it before Signal.**

### Energy Recycler

**It shuffles up to five Basic Energy from the discard into the deck.** Two copies, one more than Dancing Swords, because X-Boot pulls two out of the deck every turn and every Knock Out buries two more. Keep both types in the shuffle; X-Boot only finds two when it can find one of each.

### Switch

**It swaps your Active Pokémon with a Benched one.** One copy. Metagross ex costs 3 to retreat, and so does an Aegislash after *Metal Slash*.

### Max Rod

**It puts up to five Pokémon and Basic Energy, in any combination, from the discard into your hand.** The ACE SPEC. It is worth up to +300 late in the game, when the swords run dry. **Once per game, so hold it for the turn it makes a Knock Out.**

# Trainers — Tool & Stadium

### Brave Bangle

**If the Pokémon it is attached to has no Rule Box, its attacks do 30 more to the opponent's Active Pokémon ex.** Two copies. Five swords and a Bangle are 330, which Knocks Out Dragapult ex and everything at 330; six and a Bangle are 390, which Knocks Out Mega Venusaur ex. It does nothing on Steven's Metagross ex, which has a Rule Box, and it goes to the discard with the Doublade wearing it.

### Battle Cage

**It prevents damage counters from being placed on Benched Pokémon, both players', by effects of the opponent's attacks and Abilities.** Two copies. *Phantom Dive*, *Chaotic Pain* on your Bench, and *Cursed Blast* on a benched Pokémon all stop. It also replaces their Risky Ruins, Forest of Vitality, or Full Metal Lab.

### Full Metal Lab

**Metal Pokémon, both players', take 30 less damage from attacks.** One copy. It makes Steven's Metagross ex an effective 370. **Never play it against a Metal deck,** since it shields their Pokémon from your swings too. Counters ignore it.

# Energy

### Basic Metal Energy

Five, Mega Evolution Energies 008. It pays the [M] in *Metal Slash* and *Metal Stomp*, and X-Boot takes one a turn.

### Basic Psychic Energy

Four, Mega Evolution Energies 005. Nothing in the deck is Psychic, and nothing needs to be: Doublade's cost is all Colorless, and X-Boot attaches Psychic Energy to Metal Pokémon. The Psychic Energy is here so that X-Boot finds two a turn instead of one.

### Mist Energy

**It provides [C], and it prevents all effects of attacks from their Pokémon done to the Pokémon holding it. Damage is not an effect.** Two copies. On the Active Doublade it stops *Chaotic Pain*, *Powerful Hand*, Sleep, and Confusion. X-Boot can't find it, and nothing brings it back.

## The Thesis

The format runs on Pokémon ex worth two and three Prizes. This deck runs on a 100 HP Stage 1 worth one, and it trades.

***Weaponized Swords* does 60 for every Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash in your hand, and the cards stay there.**

| Swords in hand | Damage | With Brave Bangle | Knocks Out |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| 2 | 120 | 150 | Dreepy, Drakloak, Munkidori, Riolu, Gastly, Haunter, Drilbur, Cynthia's Gabite, Lunatone, Solrock |
| 3 | 180 | 210 | Toxtricity, Alakazam, Dhelmise, Crustle, Cynthia's Roserade, Meganium, Meowth ex |
| 4 | 240 | 270 | Genesect ex, Fezandipiti ex, Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Cinccino ex; Okidogi ex with Bangle |
| 5 | 300 | 330 | Gengar ex, N's Zoroark ex, Blissey ex; with Bangle, Dragapult ex, Mega Zygarde ex, and everything at 330 |
| 6 | 360 | 390 | Mega Lucario ex, Mega Excadrill ex, Mega Gengar ex, Mega Charizard X ex; Mega Venusaur ex with Bangle |
| 7 | 420 | 450 | Cynthia's Garchomp ex wearing Cynthia's Power Weight (400) |

**Nothing in the format resists Metal.** Lillie's Clefairy ex (190) dies to two swords, and Mega Froslass ex (310) and Mega Floette ex (250) to three, since all three are weak to it.

**Doublade has no Rule Box and no Ability.** Crustle's *Mysterious Rock Inn*, Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex, and Neutralization Zone all stop Pokémon ex or Pokémon with Abilities, and none of them touch it.

**The swords are the resource, and every exchange costs two.** The promoted Honedge takes a Doublade from your hand, and a fresh Honedge goes down behind it. Signal pays two back each turn, and Dawn, Lana's Aid, Poke Pad, and Max Rod grow the hand.

**This build's difference is the Energy, and the price is Prizes.** X-Boot never misses, and Steven's Metagross ex can swing for 200 off an empty hand. In exchange, the Bench holds two Pokémon worth two Prizes each, where [Dancing Swords](./dancing-swords.md) holds one.

## The Hard Rules

### The board

| Spot | Put here | Never |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Active | A Doublade holding two Energy | Genesect ex; Metagross ex only by [the rule](#who-attacks) |
| Bench | One Steven's Metagross ex | A second Metagross ex while the first lives |
| Bench | One Genesect ex | A second Genesect ex while the first lives |
| Bench | One Honedge, loaded by X-Boot | A second backup sword after turn two |
| Bench | A Steven's Beldum until the Metagross lands, then an open spot | Psyduck, unless they run Dusknoir |

**The target board is a Doublade in front, and Metagross ex, Genesect ex, one loaded Honedge, and an open spot behind it.** That is three swords on the board, and four Prizes on the Bench in two bodies, which is the most it should ever hold.

- **The backup stays a Honedge.** X-Boot loads it. When the Active dies, promote it, evolve it from your hand, and swing.
- **Bench a new Honedge every time one gets promoted,** since a Honedge has to sit a turn before it evolves.
- **Aegislash stays in hand** unless [Who attacks](#who-attacks) says otherwise.

### Counting swords

| Rule | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Count before you play anything.** Swords times 60, plus 30 with a Bangle against an Active Pokémon ex. | Benching a Honedge and evolving both take a sword out of the swing. |
| **Until Metagross ex lands, Signal takes it.** | One Metagross ex and a Doublade, so the Candy has a target. |
| **After that, Signal takes Aegislash,** unless the next attacker needs a Doublade. | Aegislash does nothing on the board. |
| **Dawn takes Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash** once the board is built. | +180, and the Honedge is next turn's backup. |
| **Against Judge and Unfair Stamp, the swords in hand are the target.** | Signal and X-Boot are both Abilities, so they rebuild a swing with no cards; see [Game Plan 5](#5-after-judge-or-unfair-stamp). |

### Where the Energy goes

| Rule | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| **X-Boot takes one of each, every turn.** | Two is a fresh Doublade. Taking only one wastes half the engine. |
| **X-Boot loads the backup Honedge first.** | The Active gets the turn's attachment, and the backup comes up holding two. |
| **A Doublade doesn't care which type it holds.** | Its cost is all [C]. Only *Metal Slash* and *Metal Stomp* need an [M], and X-Boot brings one every turn. |
| **Mist goes on the Active Doublade against attack effects.** | *Chaotic Pain*, *Powerful Hand*, Sleep, and Confusion. |
| **Energy Recycler after the second Knock Out.** | X-Boot drains the deck by two a turn, and Knock Outs bury two more. |
| **Retreat is Energy.** | Metagross ex and Aegislash cost 3; Doublade, Honedge, and Genesect ex cost 2. Switch costs a card instead. |

### Who attacks

| The board | Attack with | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Four swords or more in hand | Doublade, *Weaponized Swords* | 240 or more, for one Prize |
| Three swords or fewer, and 230 Knocks Out the target | Aegislash, *Metal Slash* | 230 beats 180, and X-Boot pays the four |
| The hand was just shuffled away | Aegislash or Steven's Metagross ex | neither needs swords |
| Their attacker can't Knock Out 340, and 200 does the job | Steven's Metagross ex, *Metal Stomp* | it stands and swings every turn, and saves the swords |
| Their attacker is Fire, or hits 340 and up | Doublade only | Metagross ex in front is two free Prizes |

**Metagross ex in front is a commitment.** It costs 3 to retreat, so it stays there until it dies or Switch moves it, and X-Boot keeps working from the Active Spot the whole time.

## The Energy Engine

| Source | From | To | Costs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Manual attachment | hand | anything | the turn's attachment |
| *X-Boot* | deck, one Basic Psychic and one Basic Metal | any of your Psychic and Metal Pokémon | nothing; once per Metagross ex |
| Mist Energy | hand | anything, as [C] | the turn's attachment; stays until Knocked Out |
| Hilda | deck | hand | the turn's Supporter |
| Energy Recycler | discard | deck | an Item; Basic only |
| Lana's Aid, Night Stretcher, Max Rod | discard | hand | Basic only |

**X-Boot plus the attachment is three Energy a turn,** which is a fresh Doublade and one spare. The spare goes on the backup, so two turns of X-Boot keep two attackers loaded.

**Nine Basic Energy and two Recyclers keep it fed.** About one Basic sits in the opening hand and one in the Prizes, which leaves seven for X-Boot, or three turns. Each Recycler is two and a half turns more.

# Game Plans

## 1. Turn one, going first

**No attack and no Supporter, but every Item is legal.** Poffin takes a Honedge and a Steven's Beldum, Poke Pad takes whatever is missing, and the Active Honedge gets an Energy. The Beldum has to sit this turn so the Candy works next turn.

## 2. Turn one, going second

**Dawn is the play.** It takes Genesect ex, a Doublade, and Steven's Metagross ex. Bench the Genesect, Signal for two more, bench a Steven's Beldum with Poffin, and attach.

## 3. The Candy turn

Turn two is the turn the robots show up.

1. **Signal first.** It takes Steven's Metagross ex if the hand lacks one.
2. **Supporter second.** Dawn or Petrel for the Candy if it is missing.
3. **Candy the Beldum into Metagross ex,** then X-Boot one of each onto the Active Doublade.
4. **Count, Bangle if it matters, and swing.**

**Two swords are enough to take a cradle.** Boss a Riolu, a Dreepy, or a Drilbur, and the Mega it grows into never arrives.

## 4. Rearming after a knockout

1. **Promote the loaded Honedge** and evolve it from your hand.
2. **If it isn't loaded, X-Boot it.** Two Energy, and it swings this turn.
3. **Bench a new Honedge** for next turn.
4. **Count, then swing.**

## 5. After Judge or Unfair Stamp

**This build barely notices.** Signal and X-Boot need no cards in hand.

- **Signal takes an Aegislash and a Doublade.** Evolve the Active Doublade into Aegislash, and X-Boot gives it the two more *Metal Slash* needs.
- **Or swing with Metagross ex.** 200 for [M][C][C], and Switch or a dead Doublade brings it up.
- **Next turn, back to Doublade.** Dawn, Lana's Aid, and Signal refill the hand.

## 6. The late game

1. **Energy Recycler** when X-Boot is about to miss a type.
2. **Sacred Ash** when Signal would miss, then Signal.
3. **Max Rod** when the swing needs five swords at once.
4. **Lana's Aid** on the turns between.

## 7. Choosing the stadium

| Their deck | Your Stadium |
| :--- | :--- |
| Places counters on your Bench (Dragapult, Lucky Haunt, Dusknoir) | Battle Cage |
| Deals damage, and isn't Metal (Overgrowth, Garchomp, Lucario) | Full Metal Lab |
| Is Metal (Mega Excadrill, Steven's Metagross) | Battle Cage, to replace their Full Metal Lab |
| Plays Risky Ruins or Forest of Vitality | Either one, played over theirs |

## Cards to Watch Out For
