## The Event

The [2026 Worlds Celebration tournaments](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/participate-in-a-2026-worlds-celebration-tournament-at-a-local-play-pokemon-store-get-a-pikachu-promo) run August 21-30 at Play! Pokémon stores. This is a real tournament, not open league play.

- **Format.** Standard, regulation marks H, I, and J. English prints only; the Japanese starter copies stay home.
- **Structure.** Swiss rounds, best-of-one or best-of-three at the store's choice, top cut at some locations. Age divisions: Junior, Senior, and Masters.
- **One registered 60 for the whole event.** No deck or mode swaps between rounds. The Gang's Snake Charmer swap sheet stays in the box for side games only.
- **Entry.** Free, with an optional $10 buy-in for extra prizes. Everyone gets the Pikachu promo while supplies last; each division winner gets the WINNER-stamped copy plus the themed deck box and sleeves.

**Registration details:** xero harrison, Player ID 6158695, username xer0style, Masters division. Fox registers in Juniors — dig out his player ID with the paperwork, or arrive early enough to create one at the counter. Bring both IDs, a pen, dice, and two sets of damage counters, and arrive early enough to register two players.

## If It Is the Gang

The fallback crib, since [the Gang's page](./dark.md) has no card-shop section.

Play the Prize race, not the damage race. Every body you own is worth one Prize, so an ex room needs six knockouts to your two or three. Lead the Weezing fuse, let Sinister Surge charge the bench, and close with Mind Jack once their Bench fills; count their Bench after Boss's Orders, not before. Against the 330-plus Megas your 170 ceiling means two full Weezing cycles per knockout, and that is the game working, not failing; Punk Helmet, Risky Ruins, and the Confusion coin flips make up the difference. Fighting tables are near unwinnable, so take those games loose and keep the promo. Your best seats are the other single-prize rooms, where Crazy Blast clears their whole tier in one hit.
