## Why

**Two Toxtricity means two Surges a turn.** _Sinister Surge_ reads "Once during your turn, you may use this Ability" with no "you can't use more than 1" clause, the clause Fezandipiti's _Flip the Script_ carries. Each Toxtricity on the Bench is its own Energy a turn from the deck. That is how two snakes stay fed: two Surges plus the hand attachment plus _Void Gale_'s export is four Energy a turn onto the Bench. Three Toxel and three Toxtricity are the counts that get the second one down.

**Chi-Yu is the cheap second attacker, and it beats Cornerstone.** Surge onto a Benched Chi-Yu hands it one Energy and the two damage counters _Whirling Envy_ wants, so it comes forward hitting 110 for a single Darkness. When it dies, one Energy goes to the discard instead of Seviper's three. It has no Ability, so it is the only attacker here that damages Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex (_Cornerstone Stance_ blocks attacks from Pokémon with Abilities; every other attacker in this deck has one). Two hits of 110 clear its 210. Two copies, because a one-of tech is prized one game in ten and nothing but Dawn and _Call for Family_ can find it.

**One Gengar ex, and the plain Gengar stays home.** Three Mega plus two Gengar ex plus a plain Gengar is six Stage 2 ghosts on four Gastly. At least two of them are dead cards every game, and with 3 Haunter and 3 Toxtricity that list carries 12 Evolution cards against 8 Basics. One Gengar ex keeps the Energy tax available as the second Candy when the room attaches from hand, and the Basics it frees are what fixed the mulligan rate.

**Energy Recycler is the right recovery for this engine.** Surge searches the deck, so Energy in the discard is Energy Surge cannot find. Recycler puts five back where Surge looks; Night Stretcher puts one in hand for the manual attachment. Keep the Recycler for after the first Seviper trade, when three Energy hit the discard at once.

**Air Balloon closes the retreat leak.** The Mega retreats for two, and every manual retreat sends two Darkness to the discard. On the Mega, Air Balloon makes that zero, every turn, so the wall steps back for the snake without paying. It replaces the second Switch. Jamming Tower turns it off, which is the one reason to keep the single Switch.

**One Ultra Ball, and a third Seviper in the other slot.** Ultra Ball discards two cards to find one, so the second copy left; the one that stays is the only Item that finds a Mega or the Gengar ex. The freed slot was going to be Poke Pad, which finds any non-ex Pokémon for free, but the four copies in `cards.csv` are still in the mail. The third Seviper takes it tonight: a twelfth Basic, a second snake charging behind the first, and one fewer Night Stretcher spent getting the first one back.

**Ten Energy.** Two Surges a turn pull Energy out of the deck fast, and Recycler refills it. Eleven flooded the Gang build when it replaced two Lillie's.
