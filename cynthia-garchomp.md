### Elliott's league deck · one Energy for 100 and a fresh hand, two for 260, and a 400 HP Stage 2 behind a Battle Cage

## The Thesis

**Garchomp attacks for one Energy and refills its hand while it does.** *Corkscrew Dive* costs a single [F], does 100, and lets you draw until you hold six cards. The attack is the draw engine, so the deck never has to choose between hitting and digging.

**Everything else on the Bench adds damage or finds the next Garchomp.** Each Cynthia's Roserade adds 30 to every attack your Cynthia's Pokémon make, and Premium Power Pro adds 30 more for a turn. Cynthia's Gabite's *Champion's Call* searches out any Cynthia's Pokémon, every turn, from the Bench.

**Draconic Buster is the hammer.** It costs [F][F], does 260, and sends every Energy on Garchomp to the discard. It is the attack for the Knock Out that Corkscrew can't reach. Nothing in the list accelerates Energy, so a Buster is two attachments on one Garchomp, usually one of them carried up from the Gible.

**400 HP is the defense.** Garchomp has 330, and Cynthia's Power Weight adds 70. The hardest common hit in Standard is 330, so a Weighted Garchomp survives almost anything the format swings. Four Weights means the Roserades get one too.

| Attack | Energy | Damage | With two Roserades | With two Roserades and a Pro |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| *Corkscrew Dive* | 1 | 100 | 160 | 190 |
| *Draconic Buster* | 2 | 260 | 320 | **350** |

**The Bench is the soft spot, and this list covers it.** Precious Trolley fills the Bench on turn 1, and every Basic in it is 70 HP. Battle Cage stops damage counters landing there, and the Weights put the Roserades out of reach of a 130 snipe. Those are the two cards that make this list different from the field's.

**Weakness does the rest.** Against a Fighting-weak deck every number doubles, and Corkscrew with two Roserades is 320 for one Energy. That is the whole matchup against [Lucky Haunt](#versus-the-kitchen-table).

## How It Plays

The short version, in order. [Game Plans](#game-plans) walks through each step.

1. **The Trolley turn.** Precious Trolley benches every Basic the turn wants, and Buddy-Buddy Poffin benches two. The Rocky goes on a Gible.
2. **Battle Cage before their snipe.** He runs one, so it goes down the turn before the opponent can first place counters on his Bench.
3. **Gabite, then Champion's Call every turn.** Each Gabite searches one Cynthia's Pokémon a turn. The first calls take Garchomp ex, and the next take Roserades.
4. **Garchomp on turn 3.** It comes a turn early when the lone Rare Candy shows up.
5. **Corkscrew Dive every turn.** One Energy, 100 plus 30 for each Roserade and Pro, and a hand of six.
6. **Buster to close.** Two Energy for the Knock Out Corkscrew can't reach.

# Pokémon

### Cynthia's Garchomp ex

**Stage 2 from Gabite. Gives up 2 Prize cards.** 330 HP, Retreat 0, Grass Weakness.

*Corkscrew Dive* is [F] for 100, and you may draw until you have six cards in hand. One Energy, every turn, and the hand refills as it hits.

*Draconic Buster* is [F][F] for 260, and then every Energy on Garchomp goes to the discard. That includes the Rocky, so a Buster is also the turn Garchomp stops being immune to attack effects, for good.

**Retreat 0 is the quiet stat.** Garchomp leaves the Active Spot for free, so Boss's Orders can't strand it, and a damaged one can step back to the Bench and become fuel for [Spiritomb](#cynthias-spiritomb).

Three copies. Gabite searches the next one, Ultra Ball finds one, and Night Stretcher brings back a dead one.

### Cynthia's Gible

70 HP, Fighting, Retreat 1. Four copies, all Poffin-legal, and Fighting Gong finds them too. *Rock Hurl* does 20 for [F] and rarely matters.

The Rocky goes on the Gible that becomes the first Garchomp. It shields that Pokémon from every attack effect, and the Energy rides both evolutions.

### Cynthia's Gabite

**The search engine.** 100 HP, Retreat 1.

***Champion's Call*: once during your turn, search your deck for a Cynthia's Pokémon and put it into your hand.** It reaches all six Cynthia's Pokémon in the list, Garchomp ex included, and every Gabite on the Bench calls once a turn.

Four copies, the full line under three Garchomp. A Gabite that evolves stops calling, so the second one is the Gabite that stays behind to keep searching. *Dragonslice* does 40 for [F].

### Cynthia's Roselia

70 HP, Grass, Retreat 1, Fire Weakness. Its whole job is becoming Roserade. Four copies, because the deck wants two Roserades standing every game. *Spike Sting* does 20 for [C].

### Cynthia's Roserade

**The damage.** 130 HP, Grass, Retreat 1, Fire Weakness.

***Cheer On to Glory*: attacks used by your Cynthia's Pokémon do 30 more damage to your opponent's Active Pokémon, before Weakness.** Every copy adds its own 30, so two Roserades on the Bench make Corkscrew 160 and Buster 320.

Three copies. It is the card opponents shoot first, because it is the cheapest body that adds damage. A Power Weight makes it 200, and with four Weights in the list one usually goes here. *Leaf Step* costs [G][C][C] in a deck with no Grass Energy, so it never attacks.

### Cynthia's Spiritomb

**The single-Prize attacker.** 70 HP, Darkness, Retreat 1, Grass Weakness.

***Raging Curse* is [C], and does 10 damage for each damage counter on all of your Benched Cynthia's Pokémon. Weakness doesn't apply.**

It turns the opponent's damage into yours. A Garchomp that took 260 carries 26 counters. Retreat it for free, promote Spiritomb, and *Raging Curse* is 260, plus 30 for each Roserade. Two Roserades make it 320 for one Energy, from a Basic that gives up one Prize.

**It is the answer to the Prize-tax decks.** Mega Gengar ex's *Shadowy Concealment* only discounts Knock Outs by a Pokémon ex, and Spiritomb isn't one. One copy, and Champion's Call finds it. It is Darkness, so Premium Power Pro does nothing for it and Risky Ruins never touches it.

### Meowth ex

**The Supporter on demand.** 170 HP Basic, Colorless, Fighting Weakness, Retreat 1. Gives up 2 Prize cards.

***Last-Ditch Catch*: when you play it from your hand onto your Bench, search your deck for a Supporter and put it into your hand.** It is Boss's Orders on the turn a Knock Out needs one, or Lillie's on a dead hand. It only fires from the hand, so a Meowth that Trolley benches searches nothing.

**It is the softest 2 Prizes in the list.** It is Colorless and not a Cynthia's Pokémon, so neither the Rocky nor a Power Weight can cover it, and it sits on the Bench where a gust finds it. Bench it on the turn its Supporter wins the turn, and not before.

*Tuck Tail* is [C][C][C] for 60, and puts Meowth and everything attached back in the hand, which resets the Ability. It is rarely worth three Energy.

# Trainers — Supporters

### Lillie's Determination

Three copies. Shuffle your hand into your deck and draw six, or eight while you still hold all six Prizes. Going first, a turn-2 Lillie's still draws eight.

### Boss's Orders

Three copies, and three more cards find one: Meowth ex, Petrel, and Larry's Skill. Corkscrew takes a Knock Out every turn it can reach one, and Boss's Orders picks which. Against Lucky Haunt it drags up the Mega Gengar ex, because a Knock Out on the Mega ends *Shadowy Concealment* for the rest of the game, or the Blissey ex, which is 2 Prizes that Concealment never covers.

### Larry's Skill

**Discard your hand, then search your deck for a Pokémon, a Supporter, and a Basic Energy.** One copy. It is the reset for a hand worth less than three exact cards: a Garchomp ex or the missing Gabite, Boss's Orders for next turn, and a Fighting Energy.

### Team Rocket's Petrel

**Search your deck for a Trainer card.** One copy. Supporters are Trainers too, so it finds Boss's Orders for next turn as easily as the Battle Cage, the Rare Candy, the Trolley, or a Power Weight for this one.

# Trainers — Items

### Buddy-Buddy Poffin

Four copies. Two Basics with 70 HP or less, straight to the Bench. Gible, Roselia, and Spiritomb all qualify.

### Precious Trolley

**ACE SPEC. Search your deck for any number of Basic Pokémon and put them onto your Bench.** On turn 1 that is a full Bench in one card: two or three Gible, a Roselia, and the Spiritomb. It finds Meowth ex too, but a Meowth benched this way doesn't search.

Against Risky Ruins, every non-Darkness Basic it benches takes 2 counters, so when the Ruins is up, the Cage goes down first.

### Fighting Gong

Search your deck for a Basic Fighting Energy or a Basic Fighting Pokémon. Three copies. It finds a Gible or an Energy, which covers a bad opening from either side. Rocky Fighting Energy is Special, so Gong can't find it.

### Poke Pad

Search your deck for a Pokémon without a Rule Box. Three copies. Everything but Garchomp ex and Meowth ex qualifies, Gabite and Roserade included, so a Pad is whatever Stage 1 you're missing.

### Premium Power Pro

During this turn, your Fighting Pokémon's attacks do 30 more to the Active, before Weakness. Three copies, against the field's one or two, so one boost is in hand most turns even with no Roserade up. Garchomp is Fighting; Spiritomb is Darkness and gets nothing from it.

### Ultra Ball

Discard two other cards, then search your deck for any Pokémon. One copy. It is the only Item that finds Garchomp ex or Meowth ex.

### Night Stretcher

A Pokémon or a Basic Energy card from your discard pile to your hand. One copy. A Knocked Out Garchomp comes back, and so does the Spiritomb.

### Rare Candy

One copy. Gible straight to Garchomp, on your second turn at the earliest, and never on a Gible that arrived this turn. With one Candy, the turn-2 Garchomp is a bonus, not a plan.

### Switch

One copy. Everything in the list retreats for one or zero, so Switch is for a stranded or Poisoned Active, not for the retreat bill.

### Special Red Card

**Playable only when your opponent has 3 or fewer Prize cards left.** They shuffle their hand, put it on the bottom of their deck, and draw 3. One copy. It is the comeback card, and it lands hardest on a player who holds pieces for future turns.

# Trainers — Tool & Stadium

### Cynthia's Power Weight

**The Cynthia's Pokémon this card is attached to gets +70 HP.** Four copies, one more than the field.

| Wearer | HP |
| :--- | ---: |
| Garchomp ex | **400** |
| Roserade | 200 |
| Gabite | 170 |
| Gible, Roselia, Spiritomb | 140 |

The first goes on the Garchomp in front, because 400 clears the format's 330 wall. Against a deck that snipes for exactly 130, which is *Chaotic Pain*, the next goes on a Roserade, and the Roserade survives the snipe with 70 to spare.

**It can go on early.** A Tool stays attached through evolution, so a Weight on a Roselia is a Weight on the Roserade it becomes, and the Basic is safe from a 130 snipe on the way up.

### Battle Cage

**No damage counters land on either player's Benched Pokémon from the effects of the opponent's attacks and Abilities.** Damage from attacks still lands. One copy.

Nothing in this list places counters on the opponent's Bench, so it only ever stops the opponent. Against Lucky Haunt it switches off *Chaotic Pain* on the Bench, which is the Gengar deck's whole plan, and it leaves Pain only the Active Spot. Playing it also discards their Stadium, which ends Risky Ruins.

**It is a one-shot.** Petrel finds it, and nothing in the list returns a Stadium from the discard pile. Once their Stadium replaces it, the Bench is open for the rest of the game, so the timing is in [Game Plans](#7-the-cage-once-a-game).

# Energy

### Basic Fighting Energy

Seven copies, against the field's four. Fighting Gong, Larry's Skill, and Night Stretcher all find it, so a Buster that empties a Garchomp costs Energy the deck can get back.

### Rocky Fighting Energy

**As long as it is attached, it provides Fighting Energy, and it prevents all effects of your opponent's attacks on the Fighting Pokémon holding it.** Damage isn't an effect. So it stops Poison, damage counters placed by an attack like *Chaotic Pain*, and every other attack effect, but not the damage itself.

One copy, where the field runs four. Nothing in the list finds it: Gong, Larry's, and Night Stretcher read Basic Energy, and Petrel reads Trainers. It goes on the first Gible and rides up to Garchomp. *Draconic Buster* discards it with the rest, and then it is gone for the game.

# Game Plans

## 1. The Trolley turn

Precious Trolley benches every Basic the opening wants in one card: Gibles first, a Roselia, and the Spiritomb. Poffin does two at a time, and Fighting Gong and Poke Pad fill in what it missed. Attach Rocky Fighting Energy to a Gible, because it stays on through both evolutions and makes the finished Garchomp immune to attack effects.

Leave Meowth ex in the deck. Trolley benches it without the search, and it gives up 2 Prizes from the Bench.

## 2. Gabite, then Champion's Call every turn

Evolve every Gible you can on turn 2. Each Gabite calls once a turn, and the calls go in order: Garchomp ex until one is ready to evolve, then Roserade, then Spiritomb. Keep one Gabite unevolved as long as you can. It is the search engine, and the Garchomp it becomes stops searching.

## 3. Garchomp on turn 3

The line evolves one step a turn, so the first Garchomp arrives on your third turn. The lone Rare Candy makes it turn 2. Gabite calls the Garchomp, and the Candy goes on the other Gible, which has sat a turn.

## 4. Corkscrew every turn

One Energy, 100 plus 30 for each Roserade and Pro, and a hand of six. Take the Knock Out when it's there, and let the draw find the next Boss's Orders when it isn't. Two Roserades before the first Corkscrew is the target, and three Pros cover the turns they aren't up. When the hand has no Boss's Orders, Meowth ex from the hand fetches one.

## 5. Buster for the big one

*Draconic Buster* is for the Knock Out Corkscrew can't reach: a Mega, a big ex, anything past 250 without Weakness. It discards everything, so the Garchomp that fires it needs two new Energy to Buster again, or one attachment to Corkscrew. Seven Basic Fighting make the refill easy. The Rocky is the one Energy that doesn't come back.

## 6. Spiritomb for the taxed room

Against a deck that discounts Knock Outs by Pokémon ex, Spiritomb is the attacker. The damage on your Bench is its ammunition, and a hurt Garchomp that retreats for free is the biggest pile of it.

## 7. The Cage, once a game

Battle Cage goes down the turn before the opponent can first put counters on your Bench. Against Lucky Haunt that is the turn before their first Gengar ex can attack, which is their second turn at the earliest. Play it sooner when Risky Ruins is up and the Trolley or Poffin is about to bench Gibles and Roselias under it.

It doesn't come back. Once their Stadium replaces it, the Weights are the only shield the Bench has left, so the Roserades and the unevolved Gabite want one before then.

## Versus the Kitchen Table

**Versus [Xero's Lucky Haunt](./dark-lucky.md).** This is the matchup the page exists for. Every Pokémon in it is weak to Fighting, Chansey and Blissey ex included, so every number doubles. Its plan is *Chaotic Pain*: 13 damage counters on any one of your Pokémon, Bench included, to kill the Gible line and the Roserades before they matter.

| Boosts | Corkscrew into Weakness | Knocks Out |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| None | 200 | Gastly, Toxel, Haunter, Chansey, and Toxtricity |
| One | 260 | Okidogi ex too, and a Gengar ex carrying *Sinister Surge*'s two counters |
| Two | 320 | Gengar ex and Blissey ex too |
| Three | 380 | Mega Gengar ex too |

Buster is 520 and kills anything. Here is what Xero has, and what this list does about it.

- **Battle Cage is the answer to *Chaotic Pain*.** While it stands, Pain reaches only the Active Spot, and every Gible, Gabite, Roselia, Roserade, and Spiritomb on the Bench is safe. Lucky Haunt runs two Risky Ruins and two Petrels to replace it, and the Cage can't come back, so it waits for the turn their first Gengar ex can swing.
- **Weight the Roserades.** A bare Roserade is exactly 130, one Pain. A Weighted one at 200 survives it with 70 to spare, and a Weighted Gabite at 170 survives with 40.
- **The Rocky decides which Garchomp they can Pain.** It stops the counters on the Fighting Pokémon holding it, and this list runs one. Every other Garchomp is in reach: a Poisoned *Chain-Crazed* (260) plus a Pain (130) kills a bare one, and a Weighted one survives it by ten.
- ***Shadowy Concealment*, from a Mega Gengar ex on their Bench, takes one Prize off every Knock Out your Garchomp scores on a Darkness Pokémon.** Their Gastly, Toxel, Haunter, and Toxtricity give up nothing, and a Gengar ex or a dog gives up 1. Boss's Orders and a three-boost Corkscrew or a Buster take the Mega for 2 and end it. Spiritomb isn't a Pokémon ex, so Concealment never applies to its Knock Outs.
- **Chansey and Blissey ex are Colorless, so Concealment never covers them.** Blissey ex is 2 Prizes at Retreat 4, and a two-boost Corkscrew kills it. It works from the Bench, and Boss's Orders finds it there.
- ***Fainting Spell* flips a coin when a Gengar ex is Knocked Out by damage from an attack**, and heads Knocks Out the attacker. It is an Ability, so Rocky doesn't stop it. When Spiritomb can take that Knock Out instead, the coin risks a one-Prize body, and the Gengar ex pays its full 2.
- **Meowth ex is the 2 Prizes they want most.** It is 170 and Colorless, with no Weight and no Rocky. Their Boss's Orders and *Void Gale* (230), a Poisoned dog (260), or Blissey's *Return* (180) take it in one hit. Bench it only on the turn its Supporter takes a Knock Out.
- **Risky Ruins puts 2 counters on every Gible, Roselia, and Meowth ex you bench during your turn**, and they stay through evolution. A Weighted Gible under Ruins is 120, inside one Pain, and a Garchomp grown from a chipped Gible falls to *Chain-Crazed* plus a Pain even with a Weight. Every Ruins counter on a Cynthia's Pokémon also adds 10 to *Raging Curse*.
- **A Poisoned Okidogi ex hits for 260.** That leaves a bare Garchomp at 70 and a Weighted one at 140. Retreat 0 means the damaged Garchomp steps back and feeds Spiritomb. Their answer is to Pain the Spiritomb on the Bench, and the Cage stops that.
- **Lucky Haunt runs Neo Upper Energy.** On a Stage 2 it pays a whole *Chaotic Pain* or *Void Gale*, so a Gengar ex that lands by Rare Candy attacks the same turn. That is why the Cage goes down a turn early.
- **Special Red Card comes out at their 3 Prizes left.** The Gengar player holds pieces for later turns, so the hand it sends to the bottom is worth more than the three cards it draws.

How the Gengar deck plays against this is in [Versus Cynthia's Garchomp ex](./dark-lucky.md#versus-cynthias-garchomp-ex).

**Versus [Fox's Fire decks](./blue-flame.md).** Roselia and Roserade are weak to Fire, and the Garchomp line isn't. Expect the Roserades to go first.
