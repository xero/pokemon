What this file is for.

A Standard-legal 60 that keeps Gengar and Chandelure on the same mat, rebuilt on 2026-09-23 from cards in the box for the Wednesday league at the card shop, then tested five games on TCG Live. This is the sixth build of the two-ghost deck. Phantom Toll is the v2 record and Phantom Tax is v3.1; v4.1 lived only in chat and went 0-6; v5 and v5.1 were the Live builds this page replaces, and What Changed in v6 is the full delta so nothing about them is lost.

Two things differ from every earlier version. Every card is owned, so this is a paper deck first and a Live deck second. And the room is known: sixteen players, Swiss pairings, and a field of Mega Excadrill, Dragapult, one Festival Lead player, and one Tyrantrum player. The lantern one-shots two of those four and resists the third, which is why this shell got the seat over the Darkness decks that week. Game words are defined in the table rules.

What The Research Says

Five findings shaped this 60, each one checkable.

The shop is a normal room. Per Limitless online play on 2026-09-23, Dragapult is about a fifth of the field across its builds at a 53% win rate, Mega Excadrill is 4.4% at 51%, and Festival Lead is 2.3%. Your shop plays those three plus one Tyrantrum player. Mega Chandelure is 0.5% of the ladder at 43% and Mega Gengar 0.16% at 41%, so nothing about this list comes from a winning archetype. It comes from the room.

Retreat Costs are the whole matchup table, and the snapshot in this repo strips them. Every Retreat Cost on this page was pulled live from pokemontcg.io on 2026-09-23: Mega Excadrill ex 4, Tyrantrum and Tyrunt 3, Steven's Metagross ex and Metagross 3, Metang, Genesect ex, Dipplin, and Thwackey 2, Dragapult ex, Drakloak, Munkidori, Meowth ex, and Fezandipiti ex 1, Budew 0.

Tyrantrum's extra HP is a Special Energy away. _Tyrannically Gutsy_ gives it 150 more HP while any Special Energy is attached, so it stands at 330. It retreats for 3, and one Flame makes Phantom Maze 330. The lantern resists Fighting, so _Wreak Havoc_ does 130 to it. The Tyrantrum deck that one-shots every Darkness card you own does not have an attack that kills a lantern in fewer than three turns.

Nobody plays Okidogi ex any more, and it does not matter. The card page shows zero tournament lists in the last three months. When it was played, it was a one-of beside Pecharunt ex in N's Zoroark, using Pecharunt's Ability to switch it in already Poisoned. Here it does a narrower job: the attacker with no Ability, the 260 that kills any single-prize body, and the only body that hits Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex.

Battle Cage on their side switches off Gnawing Curse. Not this deck's problem, but it decided which Gengar this deck runs. Cage prevents damage counters placed on Benched Pokémon by the opponent's Abilities, and the Temporal Forces Gengar ex's Ability places counters. Dragapult and Alakazam lists run Cage. The Mega Gengar's Ability takes Prizes away instead of placing counters, and nothing stops it.

Format check, September 2026. Standard is regulation marks H, I, and J. Every card in this list carries one of the three, and none is from the 30th Celebration set, which the shop banned for the week of 2026-09-23. The reg-H cards, meaning Okidogi ex, Crispin, Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Night Stretcher, and Prime Catcher, rotate around April 2027. The Pitch Black, Phantasmal Flames, Perfect Order, and Chaos Rising core is years from the door.

Boss's Orders [Ghetsis]

Switch in 1 of your opponent's Benched Pokémon to the Active Spot. Two copies plus Prime Catcher, and Petrel fetches either.

2. The Ferry

How one attachment a turn feeds two Megas.

_Void Gale_ must move an Energy off Gengar after it hits, and it moves any Energy. So Gengar wants plus one more aboard before its first swing, and after that every manual attachment goes onto Gengar and rides the ferry to the Bench.

  1. Turn one, Poffin for Litwick and Gastly. Attach a Darkness to the Gastly. If the hand has no Energy, Crispin or Hilda is the turn's Supporter; Crispin attaches one and banks one.
  2. Turn two, Rare Candy the Gastly into Mega Gengar; the Darkness rides up. Crispin attaches the second Darkness and puts a Psychic in hand; the manual attachment puts that Psychic on Gengar. Balloon on. _Void Gale_ for 230, and the Psychic ferries onto the Litwick or Lampent benched on turn one.
  3. Turn three, attach a Psychic to Gengar. _Void Gale_ again; the second Psychic lands beside the first. Evolve the Lampent, and Mega Chandelure is standing on the Bench with while Gengar has hit for 460.
  4. Turn four, Boss's Orders or Prime Catcher drags the heavy body forward. Gengar retreats for free, the lantern steps up, and _Phantom Maze_ collects.

Without Crispin, the first ferry carries Darkness. Gengar at exactly still swings, but the Energy it ships is a Darkness, so aim it at Okidogi or the spare Gastly, both of which want one. Game five did this twice and the dog came up holding two. The lantern then charges by hand on a turn Gengar does not attack, or off Hilda.

The rhythm to internalize. The attachment goes on Gengar. Gengar decides where it lands. The Bench never waits on a card.

3. The Gun

When the lantern comes forward, and when it goes back.

The lantern attacks when three things are true: it has , the toll table says the shot kills, and a gust or a free retreat gets it into the Active Spot this turn. Two Flames plus a gust reaches every Retreat 2 body at 330 and every Retreat 3 body at 380; check The Numbers before you commit.

After the Maze, they hit the lantern back. It is 350 HP and pays three Prizes, so do not leave it there to be two-shot. Its Balloon makes the retreat free: step back, promote Gengar, and the ferry resumes while the lantern waits for the next kill. A lantern that stays in front is a lantern volunteering half their game. The exception is a room that cannot two-shot it: Tyrantrum does 130 to it, Lopunny 160, and there the lantern stands and shoots until the third hit is coming.

If the Maze lands short, the closers are Okidogi's 260 and Void Gale's 230, in that order.

4. How To Play Okidogi

The turn-by-turn for the card with no Ability.

Get it down. Okidogi is 250 HP, so Poffin cannot fetch it. It reaches the board three ways: drawn and played, Dawn's Basic slot, or Ultra Ball. On the Bench it is a two-Prize body that pays one under Concealment; do not bench it early into a room that snipes, and never bench it when they are within two Prizes of winning unless their biggest hit is under 250.

Load it in front, with one Energy already on it. _Poisonous Musculature_ costs , so Okidogi needs one Energy of any type before it can use the attack. Attach it this turn if the ferry has not already dropped a Darkness on it; a spare Psychic is fine. Then _Musculature_: two Darkness from the deck onto Okidogi, and it is Poisoned. It now holds three Energy, which is the whole cost of _Chain-Crazed_. Poison ticks 10 at every Checkup, theirs and yours, so it stands on 230 by your next turn.

Keep it there. The Poison is the condition. Special Conditions clear the moment a Pokémon goes to the Bench, so never retreat it while it still has a target, and never leave Festival Grounds standing when Okidogi is loaded. Their Boss's Orders pulling one of your Benched Pokémon forward benches Okidogi too, and that one you cannot prevent. Cage first, Okidogi second.

Fire, and keep firing. _Chain-Crazed_ for 260, with no attachment; the Energy is already aboard. Say the target's HP out loud first. Anything at or under 260 dies now; anything at or under 490 dies to Void Gale next turn. If Okidogi survives the return hit, it hits 260 again next turn for nothing.

Check its attack before you pay its retreat. Game four on Live retreated a Poisoned dog holding three Energy to Maze a 170 HP Meowth, when _Chain-Crazed_ killed it from where the dog stood. Three Darkness went to the discard for nothing.

Let it die, or AZ's it. Under Concealment it pays one Prize and takes three Darkness to the discard, where Stretcher and Recycler want them. If it is about to die to its own Poison and has no kill, AZ's benches it, cures it, and heals 80; it comes back later with the Energy still on.

5. The Boss on the Support ex

The line the ladder taught, and it worked three games out of three.

Most Basic-heavy boxes carry a support ex that never attacks: Meowth ex for _Last-Ditch Catch_, Fezandipiti ex for _Flip the Script_, Morpeko ex for its draw. 6. The Rebuild

The first Mega will die. This is the turn after.

  1. Their attack Knocks it Out. Two or three Energy go to your discard along with the Mega.
  2. Night Stretcher returns the Mega card itself, or a Darkness, whichever the next turn needs. Hilda finds the next Mega and its Energy from the deck when the Stretcher is spent.
  3. If the dead Mega was a lantern, evolve the Lampent already on the Bench; it needs no Candy. If it was Gengar, Candy the spare Gastly or evolve the fed Haunter.
  4. Energy Recycler puts the discarded Energy back into the deck, where Crispin, Hilda, and _Musculature_ read. Play it the turn the discard holds three or more.
  5. Bring the new Mega forward with Prime Catcher, AZ's, or a Balloon retreat, and collect.

Game five ran this loop twice: Stretcher for a Psychic, Stretcher for the third Mega Chandelure, Hilda for its Energy, and the third lantern took the last two Prizes.
