# Dancing Swords

### Doublade swings for 60 a sword · Metang loads the next one · every Pokémon but two is worth a single Prize

> [!NOTE]
> **What this file is for.**
>
> Fox's Doublade deck, the Metal Maker build, which Xero is testing on [TCG Live](https://tcg.pokemon.com/en-us/tcgl/) before Fox sleeves it. Every card carries regulation mark H, I, or J. Its twin is [Robot Swords](./robot-swords.md), the same swords on Steven's Metagross ex instead of Metang.
>
> The attacker is Doublade, not Aegislash. *Weaponized Swords* counts the Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash in your hand, so the deck is a hand to hold, a board to keep small, and a discard pile to pull back from. The engine is borrowed from [Iron Excavation](./metal-excadrill.md), the Metal Maker deck. The deck list and the cards come first, then [The Thesis](#the-thesis) and [The Hard Rules](#the-hard-rules), which are how to play it. Game words are defined in [the table rules](./rules.md).

**21 + 28 + 11 = 60.** ✓ Ten Basics, nine Supporters, twelve swords, one ACE SPEC, no Special Energy, and nothing over four copies.

**Ten Basics is a 26% mulligan.** The opening seven holds a Honedge 40% of the time, and going first, a Honedge, Poffin, or Poke Pad shows up by the end of your first turn 75% of the time. It holds 1.4 swords on average, and 1.2 sit in the Prizes; three or more swords are prized in 9% of games. **Genesect ex or a Dawn is in your first nine cards 57% of the time.** Brock's Scouting had that at 64% and left for the second Switch, so it is still the deck's soft spot; see [Test and Tune](#test-and-tune). About one opening hand in five (22%) holds no Energy, and *Metal Maker* is the answer to that one.

# Pokémon

### Honedge

**70 HP Basic, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 2.** *Cut* does 10 and you will never use it.

**Four copies, two jobs.** It is the bottom of the line, and it is a sword while it sits in your hand. Every Honedge on the Bench is 60 off this turn's swing, so turn one benches two (one to evolve in the Active Spot, one behind it) and the rest stay in hand.

Poffin and Poke Pad find it, and Dawn takes it as the Basic. Under their Risky Ruins a benched Honedge starts at 50.

### Doublade

**Stage 1, 100 HP, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 2.** The Mega Evolution promo 067, Yukihiro Tada's art, mark J. It is the same card as Perfect Order 057.

***Weaponized Swords* costs [C][C].** Reveal any number of Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash from your hand, and it does 60 for each one. **Revealed, not discarded.** The cards go back into your hand and swing again next turn, so the damage is a resource you keep, not one you spend.

**Four copies.** One is the attacker, one waits in hand for the next Honedge, and the rest are swords. It has no Rule Box and no Ability, which matters more than it looks; see [The Thesis](#the-thesis).

**100 HP means it dies.** Nearly every attack in the format Knocks it Out, and that is the plan, because it is one Prize. Everything attached to it goes to the discard with it, the Energy and the Tool.

### Aegislash

**Stage 2, 150 HP, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 3.**

***Slash* does 80 for [C][C][C]. *Metal Slash* does 230 for [M][C][C][C], and this Pokémon can't attack during your next turn.**

**Four copies, and in most games none of them touches the board.** Aegislash does nothing in play that Doublade doesn't do better, so it is the purest sword in the deck. *Metallic Signal* takes it first.

**It comes out when the hand can't swing.** After Judge or Unfair Stamp, or when three swords or fewer are left, *Metal Slash*'s 230 beats *Weaponized Swords*. [When Aegislash comes out](#when-aegislash-comes-out) has the rule. At 150 it survives a bare *Aura Jab* (130), *Corkscrew Dive* with a Roserade (130), and *Solar Beam* after Resistance (110), which a Doublade doesn't.

### Beldum

**70 HP Basic, Retreat 1.** Chaos Rising 059, the print Iron Excavation runs. Its whole job is becoming Metang.

**Three copies.** Two go down on turn one and evolve on turn two. The third replaces a Metang that dies. Poffin and Poke Pad find it, and it is the cheapest Active in the deck to retreat.

### Metang

**The engine.** Stage 1, 100 HP, Retreat 2. The Temporal Forces print, or the Scarlet & Violet promo 090, which carries the same Ability. The Chaos Rising and Journey Together Metang have no Ability at all.

***Metal Maker*: once during your turn, look at the top 4 cards of your deck and attach any number of Basic Metal Energy you find there to your Pokémon in any way you like. The rest go to the bottom of the deck.**

- **It reads your Pokémon, not this Pokémon.** It loads the Active Doublade, the benched Honedge, or an Aegislash.
- **It reads Basic Metal, and every Energy in the list is one.** The cards it doesn't take go to the bottom, which is why it runs before Signal's shuffle.
- **Each Metang hits about 57% of the time** with eleven Metal in the list, up from 45% with the first list's eight, and two of them find at least one Metal four turns in five. With the turn's attachment that is two, which is exactly one fresh Doublade.

**Two in play, never fewer.** The third goes down only on a free Bench spot. *Beam* does 60 for [M][C][C] and you will never use it. 100 HP and Retreat 2 make it the card they drag up to strand; that is what Switch is for.

### Genesect ex

**220 HP Basic, Fire Weakness, Grass Resistance, Retreat 2, two Prizes.** Black Bolt 067.

***Metallic Signal*: once during your turn, search your deck for up to 2 Evolution Metal Pokémon and put them into your hand.** Doublade and Aegislash are both Evolution Metal Pokémon, so Signal is +120 damage every turn, from the Bench, for no card. It finds Metang too.

**Two copies, one in play.** It and Fezandipiti ex are the two Pokémon in the deck worth two Prizes, and every Boss's Orders they run is aimed at one of them. The second stays off the board until the first is gone.

**Signal searches the deck, never the hand or the discard.** Once the deck runs out of Doublade and Aegislash, Signal finds nothing, and from there the discard is the second hand; Lana's Aid, Night Stretcher, and Max Rod read it. *Protect Charge* does 150 for [M][M][C] and takes 30 less next turn, which is an emergency swing and nothing more.

**Finding it is the hard part.** Dawn takes it as a Basic, and Night Stretcher brings one back. Poffin and Poke Pad can't, since it has a Rule Box and 220 HP.

### Fezandipiti ex

**210 HP Basic, Fighting Weakness, two Prizes.** Shrouded Fable 038.

***Flip the Script*: once during your turn, if any of your Pokémon were Knocked Out during your opponent's last turn, draw 3 cards.** This deck loses a Pokémon nearly every turn by design, so from the first trade on it is three cards a turn, from the Bench, and it never touches the swords already in your hand.

**One copy, and it is the draw the first list didn't have.** Signal, Dawn, Poffin, and Poke Pad find Pokémon. Nothing but Petrel finds a Switch, a Stadium, or a Boss's Orders, so those arrived one card a turn. It took Psyduck's slot and the tenth Basic with it, so the mulligan rate doesn't move. *Cruel Arrow* does 100 to any one of their Pokémon for [C][C][C], and you will never pay for it.

**It costs a Prize map.** With Genesect ex and Fezandipiti both down, they can win in four Knock Outs instead of six; see [The Prize Map](#the-prize-map). Bench it once the two Metang are up, and when a Boss's Orders takes it, that was an attack that didn't take the loaded Honedge.

# Trainers — Supporters

### Dawn

**It searches the deck for a Basic, a Stage 1, and a Stage 2 Pokémon.** Three copies, and the best card in the deck.

Late in the game it takes Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash, which is +180 from one card. Early it builds the board.

| Board | Dawn takes |
| :--- | :--- |
| Turn one going second, no Genesect | Genesect ex, Doublade, Aegislash |
| Beldum benched, no Metang yet | Genesect ex or a Honedge, Metang, Aegislash |
| Both Metang up, no Fezandipiti yet | Fezandipiti ex, Doublade, Aegislash |
| Everything already up | Honedge, Doublade, Aegislash, for +180 |

### Lana's Aid

**It puts up to three Pokémon without a Rule Box or Basic Energy from the discard into your hand.** Two copies.

Every Doublade that dies puts two swords in the discard. Lana's brings three back, so it out-earns a Knock Out and lands the swords where they swing this turn. It can't take Genesect ex. Take three swords unless Energy is what the turn is missing.

### Boss's Orders

**It switches one of their Benched Pokémon into the Active Spot.** Two copies.

- **Early, take the cradle.** Two swords are 120, which kills nearly every Basic and Stage 1 a Mega grows from: Riolu (80), Dreepy (70), Drakloak (90), Drilbur (70), Gastly (70). A Mega that never evolves is three Prizes stuck in their hand.
- **Late, take the Mega that retreated.** Air Balloon and Switch pull their Mega out of reach. Boss's brings it back the turn you hold six swords.
- **On a short-sword turn, take their engine.** Toxtricity (140), Meganium (160), and Cynthia's Roserade (130) are three swords each.

> [!WARNING]
> **Leave a Mega Lucario ex in the Active Spot the turn after it used *Mega Brave*.** It can't use *Mega Brave* again that turn. Bossing a different Pokémon up sends it to the Bench, and it comes back fresh.

### Team Rocket's Petrel

**It searches the deck for a Trainer card.** One copy.

It is the Stadium toolbox, finding Battle Cage against counters or Full Metal Lab against damage, and a second way to find Max Rod, Energy Recycler, a Switch, or a Lucky Helmet. Supporters it finds, like Boss's Orders, wait for next turn. It can't find Energy.

### Hilda

**It searches the deck for an Evolution Pokémon and an Energy card.** One copy.

It is the one Supporter that finds a sword and the Energy for it in one card. Early it takes Metang and a Metal, or a Doublade and a Metal for a fresh swing, and it is the turn-one play going second when Dawn isn't there. After Judge it takes Aegislash and the Metal that starts paying for *Metal Slash*.

# Trainers — Items

### Buddy-Buddy Poffin

**Two Basic Pokémon with 70 HP or less, straight to the Bench.** Three copies. Honedge and Beldum qualify; Genesect ex and Fezandipiti ex don't.

It is a turn-one card. Late in the game it benches swords out of the deck instead of putting them in your hand, which is why there is no fourth copy.

### Poke Pad

**It searches the deck for a Pokémon without a Rule Box.** Two copies, printed Poké Pad.

It finds any sword, a Beldum, or a Metang, never Genesect ex. On turn one it takes the Honedge or Beldum the Bench is missing. Later it is +60.

### Night Stretcher

**It puts a Pokémon or a Basic Energy from the discard into your hand.** Two copies.

It is the cheap way back for a Genesect ex, which Lana's can't touch. Otherwise it is one exact sword for lethal, or the Metal that pays for *Metal Slash*.

### Energy Recycler

**It shuffles up to five Basic Energy from the discard into the deck.** One copy.

*Metal Maker* only looks at the deck. Every Knock Out buries two Metal, and by the third one the deck's Metal is thin enough that Metang misses. Recycler puts five back where it looks, so it goes down before the Makers run, not after.

### Switch

**It swaps your Active Pokémon with a Benched one.** Two copies.

A Honedge leads two games in five. The other three a Beldum, a Metang, or a Genesect ex is in front, every body in the deck retreats for two, and two Energy is a loaded Doublade. Switch rescues a Genesect ex or a Beldum that opened in the Active Spot, a Metang that got dragged up or Paralyzed, and an Aegislash after *Metal Slash*. The second copy took Brock's Scouting's slot after a game where a Metang sat in front with no Energy for four turns.

### Max Rod

**It puts up to five Pokémon and Basic Energy, in any combination, from the discard into your hand.** The ACE SPEC.

It took Precious Trolley's slot. Xero found Trolley good only on the first turn; after that, it benched swords he needed in his hand. Max Rod is the opposite card, useless early and worth up to +300 late, exactly when the swords run dry. **Once per game, so hold it for the turn it makes a Knock Out.**

# Trainers — Tool & Stadium

### Brave Bangle

**If the Pokémon it is attached to has no Rule Box, its attacks do 30 more to the opponent's Active Pokémon ex.** Two copies.

| Swords | Damage | With Bangle | What the 30 buys |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| 4 | 240 | 270 | Okidogi ex (250) |
| 5 | 300 | 330 | Dragapult ex (320), Cynthia's Garchomp ex without its Weight, Mega Sharpedo ex, Mega Lopunny ex (330), Mega Zygarde ex (310) |
| 6 | 360 | 390 | Mega Venusaur ex (380) |

It goes to the discard with the Doublade wearing it, so attach it the turn the 30 changes a breakpoint, not before. It adds nothing against a single-Prize Pokémon, and on those turns the Tool is a Lucky Helmet; see [Bangle or Helmet](#bangle-or-helmet). Jamming Tower switches it off.

### Lucky Helmet

**If the Pokémon wearing it is in the Active Spot and is damaged by an attack from their Pokémon, draw 2 cards, even if it is Knocked Out.** Two copies, Twilight Masquerade 158.

The Active Doublade gets hit every turn, so this is two cards a turn for no card and no Supporter. Attach it to the backup Honedge the turn *Metal Maker* loads it. Tools stay on through evolution, and it fires whether that Honedge dies in front as a Doublade or gets Bossed up early. Counters aren't attack damage, so *Chaotic Pain* and *Cursed Blast* don't trigger it. Jamming Tower switches it off, the same as the Bangle.

### Battle Cage

**It prevents damage counters from being placed on Benched Pokémon, both players', by effects of the opponent's attacks and Abilities.** Two copies.

*Phantom Dive*'s six counters, *Chaotic Pain* aimed at your Bench, and *Cursed Blast* on a benched Pokémon all stop. Attack damage still lands. It also replaces their Risky Ruins, Forest of Vitality, or Full Metal Lab.

### Full Metal Lab

**Metal Pokémon, both players', take 30 less damage from attacks.** Two copies.

It is the Stadium against decks that deal damage, which is most of the ladder, and it reads as 30 more HP on every body you own. A Doublade lives through *Mirage Barrage* (120) and *Cruel Arrow* (100), and with Grass Resistance it takes *Solar Beam* at 80 and lives; Aegislash lives through *Shinobi Blade* (170); Genesect ex takes *Jungle Dump* at 180 and lives. Fire still kills a Doublade through it, since Weakness doubles before the 30 comes off. **Never play it against a Metal deck.** It shields their Pokémon too, so against Mega Excadrill ex six swords drop from 360 to 330 and miss. Counters ignore it entirely.

Eleven, Mega Evolution Energies 008, the same print as Iron Excavation, and the only Energy in the list. *Metal Maker* finds it, it is the [M] in *Metal Slash*, and Energy Recycler, Lana's Aid, Night Stretcher, and Max Rod all bring it back. ## The Thesis

The format runs on Pokémon ex worth two and three Prizes. This deck runs on a 100 HP Stage 1 worth one, and it doesn't try to survive. It trades.

***Weaponized Swords* does 60 for every Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash in your hand, and the cards stay there.**

| Swords in hand | Damage | With Brave Bangle | Knocks Out |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| 2 | 120 | 150 | Dreepy, Drakloak, Munkidori, Riolu, Gastly, Haunter, Drilbur, Cynthia's Gabite, Lunatone, Solrock |
| 3 | 180 | 210 | Toxtricity, Alakazam, Dhelmise, Crustle, Cynthia's Roserade, Meganium, Meowth ex |
| 4 | 240 | 270 | Genesect ex, Fezandipiti ex, Teal Mask Ogerpon ex, Cinccino ex; Okidogi ex with Bangle |
| 5 | 300 | 330 | Gengar ex, N's Zoroark ex, Blissey ex; with Bangle, Dragapult ex, Mega Zygarde ex, and everything at 330 |
| 6 | 360 | 390 | Mega Lucario ex, Mega Excadrill ex, Mega Gengar ex, Mega Charizard X ex; Mega Venusaur ex with Bangle |
| 7 | 420 | 450 | Cynthia's Garchomp ex wearing Cynthia's Power Weight (400) |

**Nothing in the format resists Metal, and a few things are weak to it.** Lillie's Clefairy ex (190) dies to two swords, and Mega Froslass ex (310) and Mega Floette ex (250) to three. Bangle adds before Weakness doubles.

**Doublade has no Rule Box and no Ability, and that switches off half the walls in the format.** Crustle's *Mysterious Rock Inn* stops Pokémon ex. Cornerstone Mask Ogerpon ex stops Pokémon with an Ability. Neutralization Zone stops Pokémon ex. None of them touch Doublade. Mega Gengar's *Shadowy Concealment* only discounts Knock Outs by Pokémon ex, so it never discounts this deck's.

**The swords are the resource, and every exchange costs two.** When the Active Doublade dies, the Honedge behind it comes up and takes a Doublade from your hand, and a fresh Honedge goes down behind that. Signal pays exactly two back each turn. So Signal keeps the hand level, and the cards that grow it are Dawn (+3), Lana's Aid (+3 from the discard), Poke Pad (+1), and Max Rod (+5). *Flip the Script* (+3) and Lucky Helmet (+2) draw blind, and that is how the Switches, the Stadiums, and the Boss's Orders arrive.

## The Hard Rules

The card sections explain the cards. This section explains the decisions, and it is the part to read before a game.

### The board

| Spot | Put here | Never |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Active | A Doublade holding two Energy | Genesect ex or Metang; they are the engine |
| Bench | One Genesect ex | A second Genesect ex while the first lives |
| Bench | Two Metang | A third before the backup Honedge is down |
| Bench | One Honedge, loaded with Energy | A second backup sword after turn two |
| Bench | Fezandipiti ex, once both Metang are up | a second Pokémon ex before the engine is built |

**The target board is a Doublade in front, and Genesect ex, two Metang, one loaded Honedge, and Fezandipiti ex behind it.** That is three swords on the board and nine left for your hand, the discard, and the Prizes. Fezandipiti goes down last, because until the Metang are up its spot belongs to a Beldum.

- **The backup stays a Honedge.** Load it with *Metal Maker*, and don't evolve it. When the Active dies, promote the Honedge, evolve it from your hand on your turn, and swing. Pre-evolving it spends a sword a turn early for nothing.
- **Bench a new Honedge every time one gets promoted.** A Honedge has to sit a turn before it evolves, so next turn's attacker goes down this turn.
- **Aegislash stays in hand** unless [the rule](#when-aegislash-comes-out) says otherwise.
- **Turn one is the exception.** Two Honedge and two Beldum go down, because nothing evolves the turn it arrives.

### Counting swords

| Rule | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Count before you play anything.** Swords times 60, plus 30 with a Bangle against an Active Pokémon ex. | Benching a Honedge and evolving both take a sword out of the swing. Check lethal first. |
| **Signal takes Aegislash, unless the next attacker needs a Doublade.** | Aegislash does nothing on the board, so it is a pure sword. Take a Doublade when your hand has none for the Honedge that comes up next. |
| **Signal takes Metang only until two are in play.** | After that, every Signal is two swords. |
| **Dawn takes Honedge, Doublade, and Aegislash** once the board is built. | +180, and the Honedge is next turn's backup. |
| **Lana's Aid takes three swords.** | A Metal only when Energy is the thing the turn is missing. |
| **Against Judge and Unfair Stamp, the swords in hand are the target.** | Keep a Doublade loaded on the Bench, so Signal, an Aegislash, and one Energy rebuild a 230 swing; see [Game Plan 5](#5-after-judge-or-unfair-stamp). |

### Where the Energy goes

| Rule | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Run *Metal Maker* before Signal and before Dawn.** | *Metal Maker* puts what it skips on the bottom. Signal's shuffle puts those cards back where you can draw them. |
| ***Metal Maker* loads the backup Honedge first.** | The Active usually gets the turn's attachment. The backup then comes up already holding two. |
| **Energy Recycler goes before *Metal Maker*.** | It shuffles the Metal back where the Makers look. After them it feeds nothing until next turn. |
| **Don't feed Genesect ex.** | *Protect Charge* is an emergency swing. Its Energy does more on a Doublade. |
| **Retreat is Energy.** | Doublade, Honedge, Metang, and Genesect ex all cost 2, and Aegislash costs 3. Switch costs a card instead, and there are two. |

### Bangle or Helmet

The Doublade in front wears one Tool, and it goes to the discard with it.

| The next swing | The Tool |
| :--- | :--- |
| Thirty more reaches a Knock Out on an Active Pokémon ex | Brave Bangle |
| Anything else, including every single-Prize target | Lucky Helmet |

Decide from the breakpoint table in [The Thesis](#the-thesis), and put the Tool on the backup Honedge the turn *Metal Maker* loads it, so the choice is made before the turn it matters. A Helmet on a Honedge that gets Bossed up still draws two.

### When Aegislash comes out

| The hand | Do this |
| :--- | :--- |
| Four swords or more | Keep swinging with Doublade. Every Aegislash stays a sword. |
| Three swords or fewer, and 230 Knocks Out the target | Evolve the Active Doublade into Aegislash and use *Metal Slash*. It needs [M][C][C][C]: the two on the Doublade, the attachment, and a *Metal Maker* hit. |
| The hand was just shuffled away | Signal for an Aegislash and a Doublade, or Hilda for an Aegislash and a Metal. Evolve and swing. |
| Aegislash used *Metal Slash* last turn | It can't attack. Switch it out, or let its 150 HP soak a hit that would kill a Doublade. |

*Metal Slash* is 230, which beats three swords (180) and loses to four (240). Evolving also spends the Aegislash out of your hand, so at four swords Doublade is always the better swing.

## The Energy Engine

Doublade costs [C][C], so every fresh attacker needs two Energy. This is where they come from.

| Source | From | To | Costs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Manual attachment | hand | anything | the turn's attachment |
| *Metal Maker* | the top four of the deck | any of your Pokémon | nothing; Basic Metal only, about 57% a look |
| Hilda | deck | hand | the turn's Supporter; a Metal and the sword that needs it |
| Energy Recycler | discard | deck | an Item; Basic only |
| Lana's Aid, Night Stretcher, Max Rod | discard | hand | Basic only |

**Two Metang and the attachment make one Doublade a turn.** With eleven Metal, two looks find at least one four turns in five, and the attachment is the second. The first list covered the fifth turn with Ignition Energy, and the trick came up once in four games; three more Metal do the same job, and *Metal Maker* can find them.

**Late games thin the deck's Metal.** With four Metal left in 35 cards, each look hits 40% of the time. Every Knock Out buries two, so Energy Recycler comes down after the second or third, and before the Makers run.

# Game Plans

## 1. Turn one, going first

**You lost the toss, so no attack and no Supporter, but every Item is legal.** Poffin takes a Honedge and a Beldum, Poke Pad takes whatever is missing, and the Active Honedge gets a Metal. A Dawn in hand is turn two's play.

## 2. Turn one, going second

**This is the side to choose.** [Opening turns](#opening-turns) says why. **Dawn is the play.** It takes Genesect ex, a Doublade, and an Aegislash. Bench the Genesect, use Signal for two more swords, and attach to the Active Honedge. Bench two Beldum with Poffin. Nothing evolves yet.

**Without a Dawn, Hilda is the play.** It takes a Doublade and the Metal for it, and Genesect ex waits for the draw or the next Dawn.

## 3. The first swing

Turn two, the Active Honedge evolves, *Metal Maker* and the attachment give it two Energy, and the hand holds two to four swords.

**Two swords are enough to take a cradle.** Boss's Orders a Riolu, a Dreepy, a Drilbur, or a Gastly, and the Mega it grows into never arrives. Save the big swings for when the Mega is already in play.

## 4. Rearming after a knockout

The Active Doublade dies. That was always the plan.

1. ***Flip the Script* first.** Three cards before any decision.
2. **Promote the loaded Honedge.** It has sat a turn, so it evolves from your hand at once.
3. **If it isn't loaded, *Metal Maker* first, then the attachment.** Two looks and the attachment are two Energy four turns in five.
4. **Bench a new Honedge** for next turn, then the other *Metal Maker* onto it.
5. **Count, then swing.**

If there is no Honedge on the Bench, the board broke a rule. Promote a Beldum, Poke Pad a Honedge, and lose a turn rather than promoting Genesect ex or Fezandipiti ex.

**The loaded Honedge stays on the Bench until the Active dies.** Retreating into it a turn early to Cut for 40 is how the Greninja game was lost: a 70 HP body in front with two Energy and a Bangle on it, and nothing behind it.

## 5. After Judge or Unfair Stamp

The hand is gone, so the swords are gone. Two Abilities and one card rebuild it.

- **Signal first.** It takes an Aegislash and a Doublade, and it needs no card in hand.
- **Evolve the Doublade into Aegislash.** If it holds two, the attachment and a *Metal Maker* hit pay *Metal Slash* for 230.
- **Hilda is the out without Signal.** Aegislash and a Metal in one card.
- **Next turn, back to Doublade.** Dawn, Lana's Aid, and Signal refill the hand.

## 6. The late game

By the third Knock Out the deck has thinned, Signal is close to empty, and four or more swords sit in the discard. Nothing puts them back into the deck. The first list ran Sacred Ash for that, and five swords in the deck is five turns of Signal where five in the hand is a swing.

1. **Lana's Aid on the turns between.** Three swords from the discard, into the hand where they count.
2. **Max Rod when the swing needs it.** Five swords into your hand is +300 that turn.
3. **Night Stretcher for the exact one.** The sword that makes lethal, or the Genesect ex that Lana's can't take.

**Count what is left before choosing.** Max Rod works best with five swords in the discard, and it is a one-of.

## 7. Choosing the stadium

| Their deck | Your Stadium |
| :--- | :--- |
| Places counters on your Bench (Dragapult, Lucky Haunt, Dusknoir) | Battle Cage |
| Deals damage, and isn't Metal (Overgrowth, Garchomp, Lucario) | Full Metal Lab |
| Is Metal (Mega Excadrill, Steven's Metagross) | Battle Cage, to replace their Full Metal Lab |
| Plays Risky Ruins or Forest of Vitality | Either one, played over theirs |

There are two of each, and Petrel finds whichever the game needs.
